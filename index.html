Small batch, made by hand

Every bar is poured, layered and set in a home kitchen in Berhampur — not a factory line. We temper real chocolate, toast the kunafa ourselves, and fold in pistachio cream until the batch feels right, not until a timer says so.

Because each batch is small, quantities are limited. Pre-booking means you get first pick when the first batch is ready.

Why kunafa, why now

Dubai's kunafa chocolate bar — crisp toasted kunafa and pistachio cream inside a chocolate shell — has become one of the most talked-about desserts anywhere. We wanted Berhampur to be able to taste it fresh and homemade, without ordering it from across the country.

Toasted kunafa on top, pistachio cream through the middle, set in a real chocolate shell — cut fresh the day it's made.
